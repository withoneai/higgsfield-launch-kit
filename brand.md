# Ember Hill Coffee Roasters

## Who we are

A two-person roastery in a converted garage in Asheville, North Carolina. We roast on Tuesdays, ship the same week, and sell mostly to people who already make coffee at home with some care: a hand grinder, a pour-over, maybe a small espresso machine. We are not a lifestyle brand and we are not trying to look like a big one.

## Voice

Warm, plain, a little dry. We say what the coffee tastes like and where it came from. No "artisanal", no "elevate your morning", no exclamation marks. If a shot needs a caption, it should sound like a note on the counter, not an ad.

## Colors

- Kraft brown and unbleached paper: the bag itself, and most of every frame.
- Copper (#B5763A): the hill on the logo. Use it as the one warm accent: a spoon, a kettle, low sun.
- Roast brown (#2B1D14) for shadows and wood.
- Off-white (#EFE6D8) for backgrounds. Never pure white, never grey.

## Look and feel, do

- The real bag, exactly as it is: matte kraft, copper hill, the label legible and the right way up. The product is the hero of every frame.
- Natural, low, side light, like a kitchen window at 8am. Soft shadows with some direction.
- Real surfaces: worn oak, a linen towel, a ceramic cup with a thumbprint glaze, a few loose beans.
- Calm compositions with room to breathe. One subject, one or two props.

## Never

- No hands holding the bag up to the camera, no smiling models, no latte art.
- No steam effects, sparkles, splashes or beans flying through the air.
- No neon, no gradients, no glossy reflections, no studio-white void.
- Do not redraw, rename or restyle the bag or the logo. If the label is warped or misspelled, the shot is unusable.

## Where these assets go

- Shop product page (square, 1:1, and portrait 4:5 gallery images).
- Instagram feed post (4:5) and a story or reel (9:16) for the video.
- Homepage hero banner (wide, 16:9 or 21:9, with empty space on one side for a headline).
- The weekly email header (wide).

Every asset goes to a Google Drive folder first. A person reviews it there before anything is published.
